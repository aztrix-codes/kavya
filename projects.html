Current research portfolio

Four connected streams contribute to a broader environmental geoinformatics framework for groundwater contamination.

In progress

Uranium contamination assessment

National-scale probability modelling using approximately 16,756 monitoring locations and about 57 environmental predictors, with approximately 494 samples reserved for independent validation. The workflow connects prediction with exposure, monitoring adequacy and governance priority.

In progress

Fluoride spatial modelling

Cleaning and modelling of a large national dataset, including 28,185 spatially unique records, resolution comparisons and deep-learning investigation. Nearest-neighbour, bilinear and bicubic resampling are considered where resolution choices require explicit comparison.

Developing

Arsenic modelling and explainability

Research direction centred on temporal transferability, robustness, nonlinear interpretation and explainable AI. The design explicitly considers temporal mismatch and the stability of SHAP/LIME explanations.

Methodological study

Spatial downscaling

Punjab pilot using CHIRPS rainfall from 2013–2022. Planned 5 km, 1 km and 500 m outputs are evaluated with aggregation checks, Moran’s I, semivariograms and spatial-structure comparison.
