Research & methods

Groundwater research,
from evidence to action.

My PhD research integrates environmental observations, Earth observation, geospatial analysis, machine learning, validation, explainability, exposure assessment, and governance.

National-scale uranium assessment

Multi-source environmental predictors are used to model contamination probability, identify transition and risk zones, assess population exposure, and evaluate whether monitoring aligns with need.

16,756monitoring locations
494independent validation samples
57environmental predictors

Fluoride spatial modelling

The fluoride stream brings together data cleaning, spatial uniqueness checks, multi-resolution resampling and comparative modelling. Of 72,024 original records, 28,185 are spatially unique; 26,718 contain values and 1,467 are missing.

72,024original records
28,185spatially unique records
26,718records with values

Arsenic modelling and explainability

This developing stream examines temporal transferability, robustness, nonlinear relationships and the use of explainable AI. Particular care is given to temporal mismatch and whether model explanations remain stable beyond a single split.

Multi-resolution spatial downscaling

A Punjab pilot uses CHIRPS rainfall from 2013–2022 and predictor datasets ranging from 25 km to 500 m. Outputs at 5 km, 1 km and 500 m are evaluated through spatial structure, aggregation, Moran’s I and semivariogram behaviour.
