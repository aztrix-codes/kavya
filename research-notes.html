Research perspectives

Methodological principles that shape the research programme. These are thematic perspectives rather than fictional dated blog posts.

Resolution is a research decision

Changing spatial resolution can alter local variability, spatial autocorrelation and apparent model performance. Downscaling therefore requires structural validation, not only pixel-level comparison.

Validation must reflect use

Random splits alone may not represent national-scale transferability. Independent samples, temporal mismatch, aggregation and spatial pattern all matter when a model informs monitoring or governance.

Explainability needs robustness

Feature importance, SHAP and LIME are useful only when explanations are interpreted with data coverage, correlation, temporal alignment and model stability in view.

Exposure is not diagnosis

Population within a probability zone is a screening estimate. It should not be presented as confirmed individual exposure, clinical risk or disease burden.
