Decision principle

A prediction becomes actionable only when its uncertainty, exposure context and monitoring limitations remain visible.

Population-screening context

The uranium work assesses population exposure at two transparent probability thresholds. These values support screening and prioritisation, not clinical health-risk diagnosis.

1.114 billionpopulation assessed
281.13 million25.2% at or above the 0.38 screening probability

Population estimates are part of an exposure and governance framework. They are not equivalent to confirmed individual exposure, disease burden or a standalone clinical risk estimate.

What responsible use requires

Transparent thresholds

Screening and Youden-optimal thresholds are named rather than blended into a single headline estimate.

Monitoring context

Modelled risk is interpreted alongside the density, distance and coverage of observations.

Uncertainty and validation

Independent validation, spatial robustness and transferability remain part of the decision narrative.
