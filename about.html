About Kavya

An interdisciplinary environmental geoinformatics researcher working across groundwater quality, remote sensing, GIS, geospatial AI and environmental governance.

Current role

PhD Scholar in Natural & Applied Sciences at TERI School of Advanced Studies, New Delhi.

Research identity

Kavya develops AI- and Earth-observation-based frameworks for groundwater contamination assessment, spatial prediction, exposure analysis and environmental governance. Her central PhD research examines uranium, fluoride and arsenic contamination across India, with spatial downscaling and multi-resolution validation supporting the broader methodological framework.

The work is deliberately interdisciplinary. Environmental observations are combined with satellite products, climate and hydrological reanalysis, terrain, land cover, population and settlement datasets. Prediction is treated as one step in a longer chain that includes independent validation, uncertainty, explainability, exposure screening, monitoring adequacy and governance priority.
